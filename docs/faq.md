# FAQ and troubleshooting

**The cart drawer doesn't open.**
Check that **Theme settings → Cart → Cart type** is set to **Drawer**, and that the **Cart drawer** section in the **Overlays** group isn't hidden.

**Filters don't appear on collection pages.**
Install the **Search & Discovery** app and add filters there. Also check that **Enable filtering** is on in the **Collection** section. With **Filter layout: Drawer**, filters open from the **Filters** button.

**My colors didn't change everywhere.**
Colors come from color schemes. Change the scheme itself in **Theme settings → Colors**, not the section. Also check which scheme each section uses. If dark mode is on, visitors with a dark device see the **Dark mode color scheme** instead.

**The dark mode button doesn't appear.**
Set **Theme settings → Colors → Dark mode** to **Automatic with toggle button**. **Automatic** follows the device without a button.

**The popup doesn't show again after I edited it.**
The popup remembers customers who closed it. To show it to everyone again, change its **Version** field.

**The cookie banner doesn't show on my store.**
It only shows to visitors in regions where consent is required, based on **Settings → Customer privacy**. In the theme editor it's always shown, so you can style it.

**The countdown shows the wrong time.**
Set **Time zone offset** to your store's time zone, for example `+03:00`.

**The transparent header isn't transparent.**
It only works when the first section on the page is a full-width image or video, such as an Image Banner, Slideshow or Video. Check **Header → Transparent header**, and upload an **Inverse logo** if you use **Light** text.

**Animations don't play.**
Check **Theme settings → Animations**. They are also skipped when the visitor's device is set to reduce motion, when **Scroll animations on mobile** is off on phones, or when the visitor chose **Stop animations** in the accessibility panel.

**Promo tiles don't show on page 2.**
That's expected. Promo tiles only show on the first page of a collection.

**Stars don't show on product cards.**
Turn on **Theme settings → Product Card → Show product rating**, and use a reviews app that saves ratings to Shopify's standard product rating metafield.

**Complementary products are empty.**
Pick them for each product in the **Search & Discovery** app. In the cart drawer, related products are used when none are set.

**How do I get support?**
Use the [support form](./#contact). Include your store URL and the theme version, which is shown under the theme name in **Online Store → Themes**. We reply within 2 business days. See the [support policy](../SUPPORT.md) for what support covers.
