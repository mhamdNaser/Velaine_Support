# Getting started

## Install the theme

1. In Shopify admin, open **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Add Velaine from the Theme Store, or choose **Add theme → Upload zip file**.
3. Click **Customize** to open the theme editor.

## First steps in the editor

1. **Logo:** go to **Theme settings → Logo and favicon** and upload your logo, an **Inverse logo** for dark backgrounds, and a favicon.
2. **Colors:** go to **Theme settings → Colors** and adjust the four color schemes. Decide whether you want **Dark mode**.
3. **Fonts:** go to **Theme settings → Typography** and pick the body and heading fonts.
4. **Menus:** create your menus in **Online Store → Navigation**, then select them in the **Header** section (**Menu block**) and in **Theme settings → Mobile menu**.
5. **Home page:** add sections with **Add section**. Each section has a preset, so it looks complete as soon as you add it. See the [Sections library](sections-library.md).
6. **Product cards:** go to **Theme settings → Product Card**, **Cards and images** and **Badges** to set how products look in grids, and turn on **Quick add** if you want it.
7. **Cart:** go to **Theme settings → Cart** and choose a cart drawer, a cart page or a notification. Then set the free shipping threshold in the **Cart drawer** section (**Overlays** group).
8. **Social media:** add your links in **Theme settings → Social Media Icons**.
9. **Legal:** if you sell to regions that require cookie consent, check the **Cookie banner** in the **Overlays** group.

A full list of global settings is in [Global settings](global-settings.md).

## Recommended apps

- **Search & Discovery** (free, by Shopify): filters, synonyms and complementary products for recommendations and the cart drawer.
- **Shopify Forms** or **Shopify Email**: marketing emails for the customers who sign up in the email signup and popup sections.
- **Translate & Adapt** (free, by Shopify): translations for your products and content.
- A product reviews app that saves ratings to Shopify's standard rating metafields, for stars on product cards.

| Where | Recommended size |
|---|---|
| Slideshow (desktop) | 1920 × 750 px |
| Slideshow (mobile) | 760 × 298 px |
| Image Banner | 1800 × 1000 px |
| Section Grid | 620 × 410 px |
| Collection images | 800 × 800 px |
| Vendor logos | 400 × 400 px PNG |
| Shop the look, Collage, Promo grid | 1600 px wide |
| Shoppable stories | Vertical 9:16 video or image |
| 360° spin | 24–36 frames of the same size, shot at equal angles |
| Favicon | 32 × 32 px or larger, square |
