# Sections library

Every section you can add with **Add section** in the theme editor, grouped by the category it appears under. Section names match the editor.

Most newer sections share the same **Colors and spacing** settings: **Color scheme**, **Top padding** and **Bottom padding**. They aren't repeated in the tables below.

## Images

| Section | What it does | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Slideshow** | A hero slider. Each **Slide** has a desktop image (1920 × 750 px), an optional **Mobile image** (760 × 298 px), a title, text and a button. | **Auto-rotate slides**, **Change slides every**, padding, button colors and **button rounded**. |
| **Image banner** | A full-width image with a heading, subheading and button. | **Background image** (1800 × 1000 px), **Overlay opacity**, **Heading**, **Subheading**, button text, link and colors. |
| **Image with flexible text** | An image with text above, below, beside or inside it, and an optional linked product. | **Text position**, image corners, borders and padding, **Enable button**, **Select product**. |
| **Collage** | A mix of images, products, collections and videos in one layout. | **Layout** (**Large item first**, **Large item last**, **Mosaic**), **Desktop height**, **Mobile layout** (**Stacked** or **Grid**). Blocks: **Image**, **Product**, **Collection**, **Video**, each with a **Caption**. |
| **Gallery** | An image gallery. | **Layout** (**Masonry**, **Grid**, **Carousel**), **Image ratio**, columns, **Spacing between images**, **Show captions**, **Open images in a lightbox**. |
| **Section grid** | A grid of images, each with a title, text and button. | Padding, button colors and **button rounded**. Blocks: **Grid image** (620 × 410 px). |
| **Collection gallery** | A slider of collection images. | **Section title**, **Title alignment**, **Section width**, **Title font size**. Blocks: **Collection**, with an optional **Custom image**. |
| **Vendor gallery** | A slider of vendor logos that link to each vendor's products. | Title and width settings. Blocks: **Vendor**, with **Vendor Name (exact)** and **Vendor image** (400 × 400 px PNG). |
| **Logo list** | Brand or press logos. | **Layout** (**Grid** or **Scrolling**), columns, **Scroll duration**, **Pause on hover**, **Logo height**, **Grayscale until hover**. |

## Products

| Section | What it does | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Featured collection** | Products from a collection, as a grid or a slider. | Blocks: **Title**, **Collection** (**Swiper view**, **Product number**, products per row on desktop and mobile, **Show pagination row**) and **Collection URL** (a "view all" link). |
| **Collection tabs** | Several collections in tabs. Shoppers switch tabs without leaving the page. | **Tab style** (**Underline** or **Pills**), **Products to show**, columns, **Show "View all" link** and its style. Blocks: **Tab**, with a **Collection** and an optional **Tab label**. |
| **Featured product** | One product that can be bought directly on the page, with variants and quick checkout. | **Product**, **Aspect ratio**, **Show description**, **Show content in a card**. |
| **Featured product group** | One large product next to supporting product cards. | **Main Product (Large Image)**, **Heading**, **Button label**. Blocks: **Product**. |
| **Image with products** | A large image beside a short list of products. | **Image position**, **Image ratio**, subheading, heading, text, **Card style**, **Show add to cart**. Blocks: **Product**. |
| **Shop the look** | An image with product hotspots that open quick view. | **Image**, **Show product list**. Blocks: **Hotspot**, with a **Product** and its **Horizontal position** and **Vertical position**. |
| **360° spin** | A product that customers can rotate by dragging. | **Viewer ratio**, **Maximum width**, **Rotate automatically until touched**, **Rotation speed**, **Drag distance per frame**, **Reverse direction**, **Show drag hint**. Blocks: **Frame**. Add one image per frame, in order, up to 50. 24 to 36 frames shot at equal angles give a smooth spin. |
| **Product recommendations** | Related or complementary products. Works on product pages. | **Recommendation type**, **Maximum products to show**, **Slider**, **Columns on desktop**. |
| **Recently viewed** | Products the shopper viewed earlier. | **Color scheme**. |
| **Products from Same Vendor** | More products from the vendor of the current product. | **Color scheme**. |

## Text

| Section | What it does | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Rich text** | A heading, text and buttons. | **Text alignment**, **Content width**. Blocks: **Subheading**, **Heading**, **Text**, **Buttons** (up to two buttons, **Filled** or **Outline**). |
| **Icons with text** | Benefit or feature columns. | Columns, **Card style**, **Text alignment**, **Icon size**, a button. Blocks: **Column**, with a built-in **Icon** or a **Custom icon image**, a heading, text and a link. |
| **Collapsible content** | Questions and answers, or any content in expandable rows. | **Layout** (**Single column**, **Image first**, **Image second**), **Row style**, **Open first row**, **Allow multiple rows open**, **Add FAQ structured data** (helps search engines show your questions in results). Blocks: **Row**, with text or **Content from page**. |
| **Comparison table** | Compares your product or brand with others, feature by feature. | Up to four column labels, **Highlighted column**, **Striped rows**, a button. Blocks: **Row**, where each column shows a **Check mark**, **Cross**, **Text** or **None**. |
| **Counters** | Numbers that count up when they scroll into view, such as "12,500+ customers". | Columns, **Card style**, **Number size**, **Count up when visible**, **Count duration**. Blocks: **Counter**, with an **Icon**, **Number**, **Prefix**, **Suffix**, **Label** and **Text**. |
| **Animated heading** | A large heading with a motion effect. Also available as the **Scroll outline heading** preset. | **Effect**: **Typewriter** (types **Rotating words** after the heading), **Scramble in**, **Gradient sweep**, or **Outline fills on scroll**. **Main color**, **Second color**, **Heading size**, a button. |
| **Main** | A flexible section built from theme blocks. Also available as the **Rich text**, **Collapsible content** and **Features grid** presets. | See [Sections and blocks](sections-and-blocks.md#the-main-section). |

## Promotional

| Section | What it does | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Promo grid** | Campaign tiles in a flexible grid. | **Tile height**, **Spacing between tiles**, **Full width**. Blocks: **Tile**, with a **Tile width** (**Small** = one third, **Medium** = half, **Large** = two thirds, **Full row**), image, text, button, **Content position** and **Overlay opacity**. |
| **Countdown timer** | A timer for a sale or launch. | **End date**, **End time**, **Time zone offset** (for example `+03:00`), **When the timer ends** (**Show a message** or **Hide the section**), **Timer style**, **Layout** (**Center** or **Split**), background image, **Hide button when the timer ends**. |
| **Email signup** | A newsletter form. Subscribers are saved as customers tagged `newsletter`. | **Layout** (**Center**, **Image first**, **Image second**), **Show content in a card**. Blocks: **Heading**, **Text**, **Email form** (extra **Customer tags**), **Privacy note**, **Social links**. |
| **Scrolling text** | A moving strip of short messages. | **Speed**, **Direction**, **Pause on hover**, **Text size**, **Separator**. Blocks: **Item**, with text, a link and an optional small image. |
| **Announcement bar** | A message bar above the header. Usually kept in the Header group. | See [Header and menus](header-and-menus.md#announcement-bar). |

## Storytelling

| Section | What it does | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Testimonials** | Customer reviews with star ratings, author photos and a linked product. | **Display** (**Grid** or **Slider**), columns, **Card style**, **Show star rating**, **Full width**. Blocks: **Testimonial**. |
| **Timeline** | Milestones in order, such as your brand story. | **Desktop layout** (**Alternating** or **Single side**). Blocks: **Event**, with a **Year or label**, heading, text and image. |
| **Scrolling story** | Steps with text that scrolls while the image beside it stays in place and changes with each step. | **Image position on desktop**, **Image ratio**, **Image change effect** (**Fade** or **Fade and zoom**), **Show step numbers**. Blocks: **Step**. |
| **Before and after** | Two images with a slider to compare them. Also available as **Before and after full screen**. | **Layout**, **Direction** (**Horizontal** or **Vertical**), **Aspect ratio**, **Handle**, **Label style**, **Starting position**, **Animate slider on first view**. |
| **Blog posts** | Recent articles from a blog. | **Blog**, **Number of posts**, **Layout** (**Grid**, **Slider**, **Featured first**), columns, and whether to show image, date, author, reading time and excerpt. |
| **Map** | Your store address with a map, opening hours and a directions button. | **Address**, **Opening hours**, **Show directions button**, **Map source** (**Google Maps** or **Image**), **Layout**, **Map height**, **Grayscale map**. No API key is needed. |

## Video

| Section | What it does | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Video** | A Shopify-hosted, YouTube or Vimeo video with a cover image and optional overlay text. It loads when clicked, unless **Autoplay** is on. | **Shopify-hosted video**, **YouTube or Vimeo URL**, **Video description** (for screen readers), **Cover image**, **Aspect ratio**, **Autoplay** (muted), **Loop video**, **Show video controls**, overlay heading, text, button and **Overlay opacity**. |
| **Shoppable stories** | Story-style vertical videos or images, each linked to a product. Shoppers tap a thumbnail to watch and can add the product to the cart from the story. | **Thumbnail size**, **Image story duration**, **Product card color scheme**. Blocks: **Story**, with a **Video** (9:16 works best), **Image**, **Product**, or a button and link when no product is set. |

## Header and Bar

| Section | What it does | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust bar** | A row of short promises, such as free shipping or secure payment. | **Item layout**, **Mobile layout** (**Grid** or **Slider**), **Icon size**, **Show dividers**, **Top and bottom border**. Blocks: **Item**, with an icon, heading, text and link. |
| **Breadcrumbs bar** | Breadcrumb links that show where the shopper is in the store. | **hidden Breadcrumbs bar in mobile device**. |

## Pages

These are designed for page templates (about, contact, FAQ and vendors).

| Section | What it does | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| **About us page** | Your brand story. | **Main heading**, **Intro text**, **Main image**. Blocks: **Text block**, **Text Block with url**, **Image block**, **Team member**, **Call to Action**. |
| **Contact us page** | A contact page in two columns (2/3 and 1/3). | Heading, intro, button and image. Blocks: **Text block**, **Image block**, **Contact form block**, **FAQ Item**, **Customer support block**, **Map** (a Google Maps embed URL), **Account details block**. Each block has a **Block position**. |
| **FAQ Page** | Questions and answers with feature items. | **Heading**, **Intro text**, **Columns on desktop**. Blocks: **FAQ Item**, **Feature item**. |
| **Vendors list** | All vendors, grouped by first letter, linking to their products. | **Title**, **Show title**, **Show group title**, **Item In Row**. |

| Section | Page | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| **Account dashboard** and other customer sections | Customer account | Banner image, customer service text and contact links. |
| **Header**, **Footer**, **Mobile navigational bar** | Every page | See [Header and menus](header-and-menus.md) and [Footer](sections-and-blocks.md#footer). |
